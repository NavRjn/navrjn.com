## Problem

Research code on PINNs tends to be one notebook per experiment, impossible to rerun a month later. I needed to compare many PDE problems and several conditioning mechanisms quickly, and to be sure every result could be reproduced exactly.

## Research question

Can a single PINN solve a whole family of under-determined nonlinear PDEs? The thesis compares **attention-based conditioning** against **vector-set representations** for conditioning the latent space manifold of one model, with Vision Transformers as a building block.

## Approach

- **Framework is stable, problems are plugins.** Each problem lives in its own folder with an `api.py`, `problem.py` and `config.yaml`, and is discovered at runtime rather than installed.
- **The CLI is the single entry point.** `pinn train gray_scott.yaml`, `pinn viz gray_scott --last`, and `pinn add my_new_pde` to scaffold a new problem from a template.
- **Everything is a YAML config.** An experiment is one file, so a result is a config plus a commit.
- **Research to library.** The repo is packaged with automated tests, CI and Docker, to move from research code toward something others can run.

## Implemented problems

- **1D Bratu**, u_xx + λ·e^u = 0, a standard PINN benchmark.
- **2D Gray-Scott** reaction-diffusion, including latent-space interpolation videos.

The thesis is in progress (JKU Linz, expected early 2027), so I publish no benchmark numbers yet. They will go here once the comparison is finished.
